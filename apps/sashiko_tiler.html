Sashiko Pattern Tiling Tool

Use this tool to try out tiling ideas for your sashiko project. Use shift+drag to add a line. Use ctrl+click to delete a line. Drag a line to move it. Drag an endpoint to move just the endpoint. Right-click a line to change its color.
